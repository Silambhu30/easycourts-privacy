Easy Courts — Privacy Policy

App: Easy Courts (com.dscreations.easycourts) · By DS Creations · Last updated: 2026-10-31

Easy Courts is an independent app by DS Creations. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government body, court, NIC or the eCommittee of the Supreme Court of India.

What stays on your phone

Your case register, Master Data, notes, documents and app settings are stored only on your device.

Mobile number verification (SMS OTP)

When you create your account, your mobile number is verified by a one-time SMS code (OTP) using Google Firebase Authentication. Your phone number is used to confirm it is really yours and to prevent one account from being shared by many people. We do not use it for marketing.

What is sent to our servers (Google Firebase)

  • Account: your mobile number (for OTP verification) and an account id.
  • Comments only: when you send a Comment, we store your comment text and category, the name and mobile number in your profile, your account id and the app version, so the owner can reply to you.
  • Free-plan usage counters: an anonymous, hashed device id with the count of Cause List dates and Case Status uses, so free limits cannot be bypassed by reinstalling.

eCourts website

The app displays the public eCourts website (ecourts.gov.in) inside the app. You enter the CAPTCHA yourself. Case details read from the page you view are saved on your phone. Please verify information at the official site.

Payments

The Gold subscription is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card details.

What we do NOT do

We do not sell your data, we show no third-party ads, and we never store your eCourts / e-Filing passwords.

Data deletion

In the app: Settings → Delete account → DELETE MY ACCOUNT. This deletes your sign-in account and your comments from our servers and all data on your phone.

Without the app: follow the steps on the Delete account page, or e-mail dscreations1625@gmail.com. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

Children

This app is intended for advocates and case-followers and is not directed to children under 18.

Updates to this policy will be published at this same URL.
