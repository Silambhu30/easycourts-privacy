Easy Courts — Delete your account and data

App: Easy Courts (com.dscreations.easycourts) · Developer: DS Creations

You can ask us to delete your Easy Courts account and the data stored on our servers at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an e-mail to dscreations1625@gmail.com with the subject "Easy Courts - Delete my account".
  2. Write the mobile number you used to sign in to the app.
  3. We will delete your account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (mobile number and account id) in Google Firebase Authentication.
  • All Comments you sent, with your name and mobile number.

What is kept

  • The anonymous free-plan usage counter (a hashed device id with counts only, no name or number) may be kept so that free limits cannot be reset. It cannot identify you.
  • Records that Google Play keeps for subscriptions and payments are handled by Google under its own policy.

Data on your phone

Your case register, Master Data, notes and documents are stored only on your phone. To remove them, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's Settings.
